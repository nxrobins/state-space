# M6: reactive catalogs and combustion in structural worlds

Status: **Foundation. Bounded CPU tests and 8³/16³ dense/active GPU parity pass;
no 64³/128³ replay, frame or gameplay evidence exists for `ca-3d-m6`**. The
incident hold and legacy demo quarantine are untouched. [M5](3D_M5.md) retains
its own evidence; `ca-3d-m5` worlds and snapshots are unchanged by this work.

## Goal

Bring the `reactive-materials-1` catalog-driven law, including combustion,
consumed structural supports and the opt-in thermal pack (copper, ceramic, wax),
into the M5 structural world architecture, so structural worlds run reactive
materials on the CPU oracle, the dense WebGPU backend and the active-tile
WebGPU backend with exact state equality.

## Rules

A validated reactive registry passed as `registry` to
`createStateSpaceStructuralWorld3D` or `createStateSpaceStructuralWorld3DGPU`
selects rules identity `ca-3d-m6`, schema 2, world envelope
`finite-structural-1`. The snapshot's `materialCatalogHash` is the registry's
hash, which binds the rules/schema identity, the complete property catalog and
the sorted active IDs. Without a registry the world is `ca-3d-m5` exactly: the
same code path, the frozen default cold table and no combustion pass.

- The registry's 24-property cold table replaces the frozen `ca-3d-m1`
  constants in the CPU oracle and is uploaded to the GPU `cold` buffer. Inactive
  rows must be zero. Placement, validation, movement, heat, phase change,
  mechanics and names all read the bound catalog.
- Every active material must be a fuel or an oxidizer, never both, so each
  cell's combustion result depends on its own role alone. Combustion products
  must be active. Active materials keep the M1 phase set.
- The schedule is the 27 `ca-3d-m1` material passes followed by one
  `combustion` pass, then reciprocal bond normalization. Movement recorders
  observe the 27 movement/heat/phase passes; combustion moves nothing.
- Combustion is the `reactive-reactions-1` mutual pairing law over six
  neighbors. At `(x, y, z)` the direction order starts at
  `(x + 2y + 3z + tick % 6) % 6`. A fuel above its flash temperature proposes to
  its first oxidizer; an oxidizer accepts its first proposer; only mutual pairs
  react. The fuel becomes `burns_into`, the oxidizer `smoke_product`, and
  `min(floor(fuel_energy * 256 / 2), MAX_ENERGY_Q - oxidizer energyQ)` moves to
  the oxidizer. Builtin fire without adjacent fuel, next to builtin water, or
  below 80 temperature units becomes smoke. Each backend evaluates a cell from
  the immutable input alone.
- A consumed cohesive cell's product is written with the M5 solidification rule:
  cohesive solids seed integrity 255 with the fresh marker, everything else
  clears the structural word. Tick-end normalization then drops the neighbors'
  bonds, and the next support solve observes the gap. The standalone reactive
  kernel never joins produced solids; `ca-3d-m6` follows M5 here.
- Active GPU tiles stay awake while a cell is burning fuel next to an oxidizer,
  an oxidizer next to burning fuel, or fire that would extinguish. Everything
  else in the quiet predicate is unchanged.
- The plan-reuse certificate is unchanged. A consumed support changes its
  structural word and invalidates the retained plan; gas, fire and smoke changes
  away from intact cells and their contacts do not. Reuse during free fall of an
  unrooted fragment is not implemented: the certificate is positional, so it
  would need plan translation and contact prediction, which is separate work.

## Fixtures

Seven bounded fixtures in `engine/experimental/structural/structural-reactive-fixtures.ts`
run at 8³ (chunk 4) and 16³ (chunk 8) with the thermal pack registry:

| Fixture | Scene | Observed on the CPU oracle |
| --- | --- | --- |
| bridge | Wooden cantilever pinned at x = 1; x = 2 and x = 3 heated to 300 at tick 3 | Exactly two reactions; the hot ash falls away; the unsupported span falls one cell per tick to the closed floor; the pinned cell stays intact |
| sealed | Pinned stone shell on the floor, interior wood at 230 with air pockets in the top layer (8 / 72 cells) | Reactions equal the pocket count exactly (8 / 72); the chemical ledger drops by exactly 80 × 256 Q per reaction; fire becomes smoke |
| vented | Same shell without its top wall | 36 / 444 reactions, more than sealed; the same chemical identity holds |
| valve | Water column over a wax plug in ceramic walls; a copper rod carries a sustained 115-unit source | Plug melts at tick 13, water reaches the floor by tick 21, the plug refreezes after the source stops |
| barrier | Same with a ceramic rod segment | Plug stays solid wax with integrity 255 and the water does not move |
| uniform | Every cell at 200, two wood cells anchored mid-world | No heat flux or movement anywhere, yet the fuel/oxidizer pair reacts on the first tick |
| dormant | Every cell at 100, same wood | Nothing changes for 16 ticks |

`engine/experimental/structural-reactive.test.ts` also checks that a `ca-3d-m6`
world built from the nine M1 materials reproduces `ca-3d-m5` control, cut, heat
and ice trajectories coordinate for coordinate over 64 ticks; that the two
identities reject each other's snapshots and that validation needs the bound
registry; that non-registries, ambiguous fuels and nonzero inactive rows are
rejected; that odd-partition restarts and motion traces/paths work on a reacting
tick; and that every fixture keeps `driftQ` at zero after every tick with zero
structural words on ash, fire, smoke and air.

## Evidence (2026-10-31)

- CPU: `structural-reactive.test.ts` (16 tests) plus the unchanged M5, motion,
  gameplay, reactive registry and thermal pack suites: 22 files, 490 tests.
  Type check, structural TypeScript lint, Prettier, Ruff, `engine.lint` and
  `engine.generate --check` pass.
- GPU: [dense](volume-m6/m6-tiny-dense.json) and
  [active](volume-m6/m6-tiny-active.json) parity, every fixture at both sizes,
  GPU snapshot equal to the CPU oracle after every tick with zero energy drift,
  on an Apple M3 Ultra under Metal WebGPU in a desktop Chromium pane. In active
  mode the dormant fixture dispatches zero tiles and the uniform fixture reacts,
  so the reactive activity terms are both sufficient and not merely conservative.
  After the consumed supports settle, `planReused` returns to true.
- These GPU runs are development checks through
  `verifyM6GpuTiny(mode)` in the probe bundle, outside the guarded
  verification harness. `scripts/verify_volume_m5_browser.py` accepts
  `--probe m6-tiny` and `--probe m6-active-tiny` for a guarded, journaled
  receipt when a reviewed host runs it.

## Limits and follow-ups

- Qualification covers these fixtures at 8³ and 16³ only. 64³/128³ replay,
  frame and gameplay stages for `ca-3d-m6` have not run and must wait for the
  reviewed probe stage and the existing safety policy.
- The terrain renderer's palette colors only the nine M1 materials; wood, ash,
  fire, smoke and the pack materials render black until the palette reads the
  bound catalog.
- Plan reuse across free-falling unrooted fragments is unchanged from M5.
- Motion traces and paths report 28 observed passes; the combustion pass is
  outside their movement accounting by construction.
