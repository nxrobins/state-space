## Implemented law and its accounting

Each cell has an integer energy coordinate `energy_q`, measured in 1/256 units.
Its permitted range is 0 through `0x3fffffff`, with the material's chemical store
as a lower bound. Temperature is derived from that coordinate and the material's
phase interval. The packed thermal byte is a clipped display projection;
it cannot overwrite the energy coordinate or drive this law.

The ledger satisfies the exact integer identity:

`total_q = sensible_q + latent_q + chemical_q = sum(cell.energy_q)`.

The model provides:

- Four disjoint cardinal heat passes with harmonic conductivity. Each exchange
  credits and debits the same integer quantity. Neither phase energy nor
  chemical energy is added by the heat solver.
- Partial melting/boiling/freezing/condensation at a constant temperature. Phase
  identity changes only at the completed interval boundary. Heat removed during
  a reverse transition comes from its latent store.
- Registry validation for phase energy ordering, separated reverse thresholds,
  material identity on reverse edges, preserved chemical/oxidizer identity, and
  representable numeric intervals.
- Complete combustion with mutually owned fuel/oxidizer pairs, two inert
  products, and one release of the chemical store. The oxidizer receives half
  that store, bounded by its available numeric capacity; the fuel product keeps
  the remainder. No heat is lost when the displayed temperature reaches 255.
- Read-only public material registries, validated input arrays, independent
  output state, and exact fractional restart in the GPU probe.

The fixture materials in `tests/enthalpy_cases.py` are synthetic law tests. They
do not replace the authoritative production catalog or silently change its IDs.

## Quantization and physical limits

All cells currently have unit heat capacity. The units and per-pass conductivity
are not calibrated SI temperatures, energies, or time steps. This is a cellular
energy model, not a validated continuum thermodynamics solver. Phase fractions
are represented by energy within an interval, not a resolved material mixture.
The combustion rule is an abstract fuel/oxidizer conversion, not molecular
stoichiometry, radiation, or compressible gas dynamics.

For temperature difference `d` and harmonic conductivity `k`, the pair's nominal
flux is `d*k/512`. The implementation rounds this up to integer Q units and caps
it at `floor(d/2)`, available thermal/latent energy, and destination capacity.
The rounding error is less than one Q unit per exchange; the cap prevents
temperature inversion. An isolated conducting pair settles to a gap of at most
one Q unit. Conductivity zero remains insulating. Multi-cell schedules can have
small spatial residuals and a directional ordering bias; these are explicit
numerical limits, not a claim of rotational invariance.

The quotient/remainder expression avoids u32 multiplication overflow. The
maximum energy range is chosen so temperature differences and signed intermediate
subtractions fit in i32 on the GPU. Aggregate ledgers use Python integers.

## Final integration sweep

The first complete production run passed 11 of 12 commands. The Relic Line
client's newer TypeScript compiler rejected an inferred typed-array default that
compiled under the root compiler. `c3-verification-first.json` retains that
failure. Shared cold-table parameters now declare `Uint32Array` explicitly.
SS016 rejects untyped cold-table factory defaults, with positive and negative
AST checks; both client builds remain mandatory acceptance gates.

The fighter's material-only bridge initially treated the three new phase IDs
as unknown air. The new regression first failed with oil vapor mapped to air
(0) instead of oil (6). Oil vapor, molten metal, and molten glass now map to their
combat material families. A catalog-wide property requires every declared
material to import without an unknown-cell fallback. The mapping also satisfies
an exhaustive TypeScript record derived from the generated catalog, so adding a
material without an explicit mapping fails the client build. This remains an
explicitly lossy material import; it does not claim to resume engine physics.
The initial exhaustive type used computed enum keys, which the fighter compiler
could not prove covered the numeric catalog IDs (`c3-verification-bridge.json`).
Using the generated literal IDs as mapping keys preserves the exhaustive check
and compiles under that client. The targeted client build passed after correction.
